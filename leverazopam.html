Important Safety Information

Do not discontinue Leverazopam without consulting your physician. Do not skip a dose. Do not exceed the prescribed dose. Do not share.

Side effects may include drowsiness, dry mouth, sleep paralysis, memory loss, and REM atonia. Excess Leverazopam may result in coma or death.

For best results, administer before sunset.

If your child asks what it is for, consult your prescribing physician. Do not research independently.
